# Stage 1 · Taste: find the needs

The goal is a short list of real situations where they want help, in their own words. Don't ask
"what app do you want?"; most people don't know, and the answer is usually a copy of an app that
already exists. Ask about their life.

## How to interview

- One question at a time. Wait for the answer. Ask a follow-up before moving on.
- Ask about **situations**, not schedules. "When does this come up?" works better than "walk me
  through your day". Most needs have no fixed time; they are triggered by something ("when I
  find a video I want to share", "after an interview", "when a form asks for my address again").
- Ask for the last time it happened. Specific stories beat general wishes.
- Ask what they do **today, without AI**. That is the baseline the dish has to beat, and sometimes
  the honest answer is that a spreadsheet already does the job.
- Ask which step **must stay theirs**. People know exactly which moment they would never hand over
  ("I always press send myself").
- Don't sell. If a need is better solved without AI, say so.

## Questions to draw from

Start broad, then follow what they say.

1. What do you do on your computer that feels like the same steps every time?
2. What did you last put off because it was tedious, not because it was hard?
3. When did you last copy something from one place to another by hand? What was it?
4. What do you keep explaining again to people or tools ("here is my background again…")?
5. Is there something you'd like to make or share, but the effort stops you?
6. What do you wish you remembered better about your own work or past?
7. For each one: when does it come up? What do you do now? How long does it take?
   Which part do you want to keep doing yourself?

## The needs map

Write `kitchen/needs-map.md` in their language and show it to them. Change it until they agree.

```markdown
# Needs map · <name> · <date>

## <short name of the need>
- **When it comes up:** <the situation that triggers it>
- **What I do today:** <steps, tools, time it takes>
- **What I want help with:** <the part they want the assistant to do>
- **What stays mine:** <the decisions and clicks they keep>
- **Data it needs:** <files, accounts, notes; and where they live>
- **Is AI needed?** <yes / partly / no, and why>

## …
```

## Picking the first dish

Choose together. A good first dish:

- comes up often, so they will use it this week;
- needs no account on someone else's site;
- can be built in one session.

If nothing on the map fits, serve the voice-clone dish first (stage 4). It works for almost
anyone and shows the whole kitchen working.
